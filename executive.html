Overview

Built to coordinate the project, not just participate in it.

Executive Custom Construction serves as the general contracting and project management arm within Executive Companies, helping move work forward through planning, coordination, and field leadership.

Executive Custom Construction is positioned to lead projects with a practical, execution-focused approach. The value is not just in managing paperwork or schedules independently, but in coordinating the work in a way that keeps the project moving cleanly across phases and trades.

From preconstruction planning to closeout, the role of ECC is to support communication, maintain project direction, and keep jobsite execution aligned with the broader goals of the owner and team.

As part of Executive Companies, ECC also benefits from a connected structure that includes excavation and coatings support, helping create a stronger path through the work when multiple scopes need to stay coordinated.
