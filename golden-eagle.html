Overview

Built to support the work that starts the project right.

Golden Eagle Excavation serves as the excavation and sitework division within Executive Companies, bringing practical field support to the early phases that set the pace for the rest of the project.

Golden Eagle Excavation is positioned to support the foundational stages of construction with a focus on site readiness, underground work, and field execution that helps projects move forward cleanly.

Early-phase work often sets the tone for the rest of the build. Golden Eagle is built to support that phase with practical coordination, responsiveness, and an understanding of how excavation and utility work affect the larger construction schedule.

As part of Executive Companies, Golden Eagle Excavation also benefits from a broader structure that supports coordination when projects involve connected sitework, general contracting, and finish scopes.
