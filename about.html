About Executive Companies

Built on work ethic, relationships, and service.

Executive Companies was built with a simple goal: create a group that can serve clients well, build quality work, and stay grounded in something bigger than the job itself. Our history is rooted in construction, but our purpose also includes the people and communities around us.

What matters to us

We believe good work should create more than finished projects. It should build trust, open doors for others, and leave a positive mark on the community we live in.

Our group brings together construction, excavation, and interior scopes under one coordinated umbrella, with a long-term focus on quality, relationships, and giving back.

Our story

How the company grew

This page is designed so you can tell your story in a more personal way than the homepage. The wording below is polished, but easy to swap once you want to tighten the details around your exact history.

Executive Companies began with a commitment to do work the right way — with strong communication, dependable follow-through, and a level of care that clients can feel throughout the project. Over time, that commitment grew into a broader vision: bring together the right people, the right scopes, and the right structure to better serve clients from the ground up.

As the company evolved, the group expanded to include specialized capabilities across general construction, excavation, and interior build scopes. That growth was not just about adding services. It was about building a more coordinated team that could create cleaner handoffs, stronger accountability, and better overall project outcomes.

Today, Executive Companies reflects that larger vision. Each company within the group has its own strengths, but all of them work toward the same goal: delivering quality work, building long-term relationships, and representing the business with integrity.

The group today

  • Executive Custom Construction
    General contracting, coordination, and project delivery.
  • Apex Coatings
    Framing, drywall, and finish-related scopes.
  • Golden Eagle Excavation
    Excavation, utilities, grading, and concrete-related site services.
