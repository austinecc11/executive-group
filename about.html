About Executive Companies

Built in Colorado Springs. Built to last.

Executive Companies is a family-owned business rooted in Colorado Springs since 1975. Founded by Gary Erickson, who began as a residential framer, the company was built through decades of hard work, strong relationships, and a commitment to doing quality work the right way. What started in the field has grown into a coordinated group spanning construction, excavation, and interior scopes — while still holding onto the same values that built the company from the beginning.

Since 1975

From residential framing to a multi-scope construction group, our story is still grounded in craftsmanship, trust, and long-term commitment to the community.

Family-owned. Colorado Springs based. Built on nearly 50 years of experience.

Our mission

Why we do what we do

Our mission is to build with excellence, serve with integrity, and create lasting value for the clients and communities we work with. We want every project to reflect strong communication, dependable execution, and a level of care that people can trust. Just as importantly, we want the company itself to stand for something meaningful — supporting our team, strengthening relationships, and making a positive impact in Colorado Springs through both our work and our charitable focus.
