About Executive Companies

Built in Colorado Springs. Built to last.

Executive Companies is a family-owned business rooted in Colorado Springs since 1975. Founded by Gary Erickson, who began as a residential framer, the company was built through decades of hard work, strong relationships, and a commitment to doing quality work the right way. What started in the field has grown into a coordinated group spanning construction, excavation, and interior scopes — while still holding onto the same values that built the company from the beginning.

Our mission

Our mission is to deliver well-executed, high-quality projects through strong communication, dependable execution, and attention to detail at every phase of the project. We aim to build lasting relationships with our clients and partners while growing a company that reflects integrity, consistency, and a long-term commitment to the Colorado Springs community.

Community & giving

As a company built in Colorado Springs, we believe our work should have an impact beyond the jobsite. We actively support organizations like Springs Rescue Mission and other faith-based groups that are making a difference in our community. Our goal is to continue building a company that not only delivers quality projects, but also contributes in a meaningful way to the people and organizations around us.
