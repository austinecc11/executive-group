About Executive Companies

Built in Colorado Springs. Built to last.

Family-owned in Colorado Springs since 1975

Executive Companies is a family-owned business rooted in Colorado Springs since 1975. Founded by Gary Erickson, who began as a residential framer, the company was built through decades of hard work, strong relationships, and a commitment to doing quality work the right way. What started in the field has grown into a coordinated group spanning construction, excavation, and interior scopes — while still holding onto the same values that built the company from the beginning.

Our story

From the field to the company we are today

Gary Erickson started in residential framing, building the company the same way many strong businesses begin — through steady work, consistency, and a reputation earned over time. As the years went on, that foundation expanded into something bigger: a coordinated group with the ability to serve clients across construction, excavation, and interior scopes while still operating with the same mindset it was built on from the beginning. Today, Executive Companies continues to grow as a family-owned business with deep roots in Colorado Springs and a long-term commitment to doing quality work the right way.

Our mission

Why we do what we do

Our mission is to deliver high-quality work through strong communication, dependable execution, and attention to detail at every phase of the project. We aim to build lasting relationships with our clients and partners while growing a company that reflects integrity, consistency, and a long-term commitment to the Colorado Springs community.

What that means

Clear expectations, dependable follow-through, and a standard of work that reflects well on the people behind it.

Community & giving

Supporting the community we’re part of

As a company built in Colorado Springs, we believe our work should have an impact beyond the jobsite. We actively support organizations like Springs Rescue Mission and other faith-based groups that are making a difference in our community. Our goal is to continue growing a company that not only builds quality projects, but also contributes in a meaningful way to the people and organizations around us.

  • Support for Springs Rescue Mission
  • Partnership with faith-based organizations
  • A long-term commitment to local impact
